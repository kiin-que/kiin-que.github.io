These two books whisper of encounters and connection, of love and growth.
And at the same time, they simply show us loss and change.

Some of us weep at beautiful meetings and farewells. Others remain composed.

Is it nostalgia for a time we cannot return to, a tenderness toward ourselves?
Or is it anticipation of what lies ahead, and an expression of relief?

Do we live this way—forming bonds and, inevitably, being hurt?
We are so slight, so fragile. Yet we keep a quiet, compassionate gaze on one another, and that makes us seem great.
And so… so… I am glad.
